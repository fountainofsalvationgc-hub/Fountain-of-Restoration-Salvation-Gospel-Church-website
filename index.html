Welcome

At Fountain of Restoration and Salvation, we believe that no matter where you have been or what you have walked through, God’s grace is ready to restore your joy, renew your strength, and anchor your soul in His salvation.

We are committed to preaching the Gospel, winning souls, prayer and worship, teaching the Word of God, and bringing divine restoration to lives and families.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

To be a life-giving fountain where the broken are restored through the Word of God, the lost are saved by the Gospel of Jesus Christ, and all are empowered to live a purposeful life.

Vision

To see a transformed community where every individual experiences total restoration in spirit, soul, and body, overflowing with the love and salvation of Christ.

Our Core Values

Fountain of Life

A life-giving source where people come to drink from the never-ending well of God’s presence, love, and Holy Spirit empowerment.

Restoration of the Total Man

God’s power to heal, rebuild, and recover every area of life spiritually, emotionally, mentally, and physically.

Salvation as our Priority

A passion for soul-winning, rescuing the lost and guiding people into eternal life through Jesus Christ.

Gospel in Word and Power

Uncompromised truth of God’s Word, backed by the work and power of the Holy Spirit.

Church as a Family

A loving, unified community where believers are discipled, cared for, and equipped to fulfill their God-given purpose.

Fountain of Restoration & Salvation Gospel Church (USA)

Fountain of Restoration and Salvation Gospel Church (USA) is the United States branch of Fountain of Restoration and Salvation Gospel Church, Nigeria. The global ministry is led by the Senior Pastor, Pastor Oshodi-Glover Muyiwa, while the USA branch operates under the leadership and spiritual oversight of Pastor (Mrs) Bukola Taiwo. Together, the ministry is dedicated to preaching the Gospel, winning souls, and bringing divine restoration to lives.

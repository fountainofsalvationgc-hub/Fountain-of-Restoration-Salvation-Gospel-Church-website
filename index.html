Welcome

At Fountain of Restoration and Salvation, we believe that no matter where you have been or what you have walked through, God’s grace is ready to restore your joy, renew your strength, and anchor your soul in His salvation.

We are committed to preaching the Gospel, winning souls, prayer and worship, teaching the Word of God, and bringing divine restoration to lives and families.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

To be a life-giving fountain where the broken are restored through the Word of God, the lost are saved by the Gospel of Jesus Christ, and all are empowered to live a purposeful life.

Vision

To see a transformed community where every individual experiences total restoration in spirit, soul, and body, overflowing with the love and salvation of Christ.

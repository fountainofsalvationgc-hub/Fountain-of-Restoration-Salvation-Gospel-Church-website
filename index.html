OUR STORY

Built to be a fountain of life, restoration and salvation.

Fountain of Restoration and Salvation Gospel Church is a Christian Gospel church committed to the Word of God, prayer, soul-winning, spiritual growth, fellowship and the restoration of lives.

01

Our Heritage

Founded by Late Pastor (Mrs) Victoria Oshodi-Glover, the ministry has a heritage of faith, service and commitment to the Gospel.

02

A New Name, Same Gospel

In 2026, the church changed its name from Fountain of Salvation Gospel Church to Fountain of Restoration and Salvation Gospel Church, expressing its calling toward total restoration while remaining rooted in salvation through Christ.

03

Our Global Family

The ministry has a branch in the United States of America, extending the same Gospel mission across nations.

OUR MISSION

To be a life-giving fountain.

To be a life-giving fountain where the broken are restored through the Word of God, the lost are saved by the Gospel of Jesus Christ, and all are empowered to live a purposeful life.

OUR VISION

A transformed community.

To see a transformed community where every individual experiences total restoration in spirit, soul, and body, overflowing with the love and salvation of Christ.

WHAT GUIDES US

Our Core Values

01

Fountain of Life

We are a life-giving source where people come to drink from the never-ending well of God's presence, love, and Holy Spirit empowerment.

02

Restoration of the Total Man

We believe in God's power to heal, rebuild, and recover every area of an individual's life—spiritually, emotionally, mentally, and physically.

03

Salvation as our Priority

We are driven by a passion for soul-winning, dedicated to rescuing the lost and guiding them into eternal life through Jesus Christ.

04

Gospel in Word and Power

We are committed to preaching and teaching the uncompromised truth of God's Word, backed by the signs, wonders, and miracles of the Holy Spirit.

05

Church as a Family

We cultivate a loving, unified, and covenant-bound community where every believer is discipled, cared for, and equipped to fulfill their destiny.

OUR FOUNDATION

What We Believe

Our statement of faith is centered on the Bible, the Gospel of Jesus Christ and the work of the Holy Spirit.

✦

The Bible

We believe the Bible is God's perfect Word and our ultimate guide for living.

✦

The Trinity

We believe in one true God who exists eternally as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

✦

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus is God in the flesh, who died for our sins, rose from the dead, and is coming back again.

✦

Salvation

We believe salvation is a free gift of grace received through faith in Jesus Christ alone, cleansing us from sin and giving us eternal life.

✦

Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit lives inside every believer, giving us power to live a holy life and serve God effectively.

✦

Restoration

We believe God's power is available today to heal the sick, deliver the oppressed, and fully restore broken lives and families.

✦

The Church

We believe the church is God's family on earth, called to love one another and take the Gospel to the ends of the earth.

INTERNATIONAL BRANCH

Fountain of Restoration & Salvation Gospel Church (USA)

Fountain of Restoration and Salvation Gospel Church (USA) is the United States branch of Fountain of Restoration and Salvation Gospel Church, Nigeria. The global ministry is led by the Senior Pastor, Pastor Oshodi-Glover Muyiwa, while the USA branch operates under the leadership and spiritual oversight of Pastor (Mrs) Bukola Taiwo.

Together, the ministry is dedicated to preaching the Gospel, winning souls, and bringing divine restoration to lives.
